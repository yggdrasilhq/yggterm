## SECTION 4 — WHAT WILL SAVE YOU TIME

- ⛔ **§5's Force half cannot be manufactured headlessly, and must not be forced
  on a live host.** It needs an all-interruptible blocker set, i.e. an agent-kind
  session with no plain shell beside it. `server attach` mints a shell (exempt —
  it vetoes the force and hides the state under test); `automation run` routes
  through app-control and refuses where there is no GUI. The two honest routes
  are catching it in the wild, or a full GUI inside
  `scripts/underglass-sandbox.sh`'s private sway + private `YGGTERM_HOME`.
- ⭐ **A sandbox daemon is cheap and answers most of these questions.** Copy the
  release binary, `YGGTERM_HOME=<scratch>`, `server daemon`, then
  `server attach <name> /tmp --allow-plain-shell-fallback` for an owned session.
  To make it owe a swap: `mv` a wrapper over its binary that reports a newer
  `--version` and exits on `server daemon` — the "successor that never arrives".
  `YGGTERM_DISABLE_SELF_RETIRE_HANDOFF=1` sends it to the cold gate instead.
- ⛔ **A daemon FREEZES its launch environment and every session it later spawns
  inherits it.** Never start one from an ssh shell. Read `/proc/<gui>/environ`
  and launch with that. This is in the queue entry as part of the repair recipe.
- ⚠ **The deadline's clock only starts where the swap lane gives up.** With a
  queue entry present the drainer returns `Lingering` and the cold gate — and so
  §5 — is never evaluated. Its real trigger is `newer_daemon_live`, empty hands,
  or the handoff kill-switch.
- ⚠ **Adding a `ServerRequest`/`ServerResponse` variant fails
  `protocol_shape_stamp_forces_version_bump`.** That is the test doing its job:
  allocate a version with `scripts/bump-version.sh` (it pushes the bump ALONE and
  FIRST), then update `STAMPED_AT_VERSION` + `STAMPED_SHAPE_HASH` — the failure
  message prints the hash to paste.
- ⚠ **`role_gate` is an exhaustive match with no wildcard**, so a new variant
  will not compile until you classify its shadow access. Classify by SCOPE, not
  by whether it mutates.
- ⚠ **TWO pre-existing flakes, both load-sensitive, neither yours.**
  `tests::local_cc_relaunch_rebuild_collapses_poisoned_identity_to_row_id`
  (order/parallelism), and
  `terminal::tests::terminal_write_queue_reports_backpressure_without_blocking_request_thread`,
  which asserts `elapsed() < 100ms` and fails on a machine busy with builds and
  sandboxes — passed 3/3 in isolation and in the next full run. Reproduce on a
  quiet tree before believing either.
- ⛔⛔ **NEVER pipe `cargo test` through `tail`.** `| tail` returns TAIL's exit
  code, so a red suite reads as green and an `&&` chain commits and pushes on
  top of it. I did exactly that today and shipped a commit over a failing
  protocol-stamp test. Redirect to a file, check `$?`, then grep.
